Our story

In our fictional origin story, Zachariah, Branson, and Grace needed a custom part for a class project. They designed and 3D-printed it, inspiring them to start Precision Prints.

Today, our imaginary campus workshop helps students and makers turn their ideas into affordable, useful products.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a digital model?

No! Bring a sketch and measurements, and ask about our design help.

How long does printing take?

Our fictional turnaround is 2–4 school days for small approved designs.

Can you print my homework?

We can print your project model, but the calculations are still yours!
